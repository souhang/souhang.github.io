## About Me

I am Hang Su, a theoretical physicist working on
quantum many-body physics and condensed matter theory.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics from
Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research focuses on quantum spin systems,
Lieb-Schultz-Mattis theorems, quantum many-body
physics, and numerical methods.

- Quantum Information and Open Quantum Systems
- Quantum Monte Carlo Methods
