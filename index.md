## About Me

I am a theoretical physicist working on quantum many-body physics,
condensed matter theory, and quantum information.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics from Shanghai Jiao Tong University
in 2026, under the supervision of Prof. Yuan Yao.

My research focuses on quantum spin systems, Lieb-Schultz-Mattis
theorems, symmetry-protected topological phases, and numerical
methods for strongly correlated quantum systems.

I am also interested in quantum information, open quantum systems,
and quantum transport.

---

## Research Interests

- Quantum Many-Body Physics and Quantum Magnetism
- Lieb-Schultz-Mattis Theorems and Topological Phases
- Quantum Information and Open Quantum Systems
- Quantum Monte Carlo and Numerical Methods
